# Privacy Policy — Impulsive

**Effective date:** April 6, 2026
**Last updated:** April 6, 2026

---

## The short version

Impulsive does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

---

## What data Impulsive stores

Impulsive stores the following data **locally on your device only**, inside an encrypted Room database:

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Your baseline session count | Drives the tapering schedule |
| Selected triggers (e.g. Bored, Stressed) | Routes you to the right intervention |
| Selected identity anchors | Personalises your path |
| Selected path (Psychological / Spiritual) | Configures your experience |
| Session logs (date, trigger, outcome) | Powers the Focus screen chart |
| Weekly targets and tapering history | Tracks your progress over time |
| Bypass and relapse events | Supports the relapse protocol |
| Evaluation metrics | Included in your optional data export |

No names, emails, phone numbers, device identifiers, or location data are ever stored.

---

## What Impulsive does NOT do

- Does not connect to the internet
- Does not send data to any server
- Does not use analytics or crash reporting services
- Does not use advertising SDKs
- Does not create an account
- Does not share any data with third parties — ever

---

## Permissions used

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| `PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS` | Detects when a monitored app is opened so the intercept screen can appear |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Shows the monitor foreground service notification and session timer |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Keeps the monitor and session timer running while in use |
| `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS` | Prevents Android from killing the monitor service in the background |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Restarts the weekly check-in worker after a device reboot |

None of these permissions are used to collect data about you.

---

## Your data export

Impulsive includes a voluntary JSON export feature. When you use it, a file is saved to your device's Downloads folder. This file never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself.

---

## Data deletion

To delete all Impulsive data, go to **Android Settings → Apps → Impulsive → Storage → Clear Data**. This permanently removes everything the app has stored.

---

## Children

Impulsive is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL. Impulsive's core commitment — no data leaves your device — will never change.
