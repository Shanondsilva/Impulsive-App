# V28 future platform roadmap

Everything in this document is planned research and development only. None of it is implemented by the current Android application.

## Private Learning Network

Possible future work could explore federated or privacy-preserving aggregate learning across consenting installations.

Required conditions:

- It is not implemented.
- It requires specialist privacy and security research.
- It requires sufficient consenting users before aggregate learning could be meaningful.
- It requires threat modelling, independent security review, data-protection review, and explicit opt-in design.
- Raw behavioural content, plan text, protected-source details, journal content, and identifiable event histories must remain local.
- Aggregate output would need robust minimum cohort sizes, contribution bounds, deletion semantics, and resistance to reconstruction.

No current code uploads adaptive behavioural records or trains a central behavioural model.

## Moment Protocol Studio

A possible future controlled authoring system could manage approved support protocols and versioned safety constraints.

Required conditions:

- It is not implemented.
- It requires protocol governance and controlled publishing.
- It requires appropriate expert partnerships.
- It requires safety validation before any protocol is exposed to users.
- It requires commercial validation.
- It must not permit arbitrary executable actions, unrestricted intents, or unreviewed health claims.

There is no Protocol Studio runtime, marketplace, clinician marketplace, or expert marketplace in the current application.

## Organisation and research partnerships

Organisation or research collaboration is a possible future route only.

- No partnership is claimed unless supported by separate executed evidence.
- No clinical claim is made.
- No health-provider adoption is claimed.
- No customer, university, employer, public-sector, or research partner is implied by this roadmap.
- Any research would require appropriate ethics, consent, privacy, security, governance, and publication review.

## Explicitly excluded from current scope

The current implementation does not include federated learning, central machine-learning training, cloud behavioural models, organisation dashboards, medical prediction, emotion recognition, passive diagnosis, camera or microphone analysis, large-language-model coaching, clinical recommendations, or treatment claims.

## V28 foundation now available

The local engineering foundation now includes versioned intervention contracts, decision evidence passports, exact plan revisions, evidence-quality tiers, explanation receipts, route-sensitive screen privacy, bounded retention, adaptive schema-2 recovery, and a debug-only replay laboratory. These are platform primitives, not proof that any roadmap product or partnership exists.

Future work must reuse the same privacy and evidence boundaries: no protected-source identity in adaptive records, explicit protocol versions, assignment separate from actual choice, no guessing missing historical context, strict authenticated recovery ownership, and user-controlled deletion.

## Readiness gates before roadmap expansion

- Complete all 56 manual tests and retain device evidence.
- Review the technical invention disclosure with qualified counsel before making intellectual-property statements.
- Conduct threat modelling and accessibility review for any new protocol.
- Define an explicit consent, governance, and deletion model before any aggregate or organisation feature.
- Keep passive diagnosis, clinical systems, uploaded behavioural models, and autonomous coaching excluded unless separately approved and evidenced.

## PathShift platform status

PathShift is now a local, transparent closed-loop primitive: one current estimate, one active cycle, current review, privacy controls, exact preparation lineage, and existing character scene. Multi-cycle comparison, advanced preparation history, and longer Plus browsing remain later commercial configuration. No claim is made that Plus predicts more accurately, and no LP multiplier is planned.

Before comparison features, require conservative cross-cycle evidence thresholds, comprehension research, accessibility evidence, DPIA/legal review, and completion of manual tests 1 through 105.
# Protection Coach future packaging

Future work may connect accepted Protection Coach recommendations to broader Plus packaging for multiple schedules, multiple Moment Plans, advanced PathShift history, and encrypted recovery. v28 intentionally limits this package to calm promotion surfaces and does not silently change entitlement access, prices, LP, prediction claims, or support completion.
