# v28 Onboarding-to-Action Policy

The onboarding bridge maps broad setup answers into visible suggestions that the user can review, accept, edit, or skip.

Required user-facing copy:

`These are suggestions based on your answers. You can change them now or later.`

## Mapping

- Social media: review installed social apps and open the protected-app selector; no app is protected automatically.
- Browser/search/browsing: review Website Protection and browser guidance; no browsing history or search term is stored.
- Boredom: make Pivot Game, Reset Reading, and Moment Plan support easier to find.
- Stress, difficult thoughts, or being alone: emphasize Short Pause, Moment Plan, Reset Reading, and Journal only when deliberately selected.
- Late night or sleep difficulty: review an evening protection window and practise an evening Moment Plan.
- After waking: review a morning protection window and practise a morning Moment Plan.
- Week-one goals: Home emphasis only; goals are not outcome evidence.

## Control model

Nothing becomes active before confirmation. Reopening suggested setup must not duplicate apps, plans, schedules, or ledger rows.
