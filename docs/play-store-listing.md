## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Most apps want your attention. Impulsive helps you take it back.

Impulsive is a private, on-device tool for people who want to spend less time on social media, streaming, and other high-dopamine apps — not through willpower alone, but through structured friction, reflection, and gradual reduction.

HOW IT WORKS

When you open a monitored app — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, and more — Impulsive intercepts you before you enter. You see a full-screen pause. To continue, you hold for 15 seconds. That moment of resistance is the whole point.

During the hold, you can choose to walk away. If you do, Impulsive logs it. That counts.

If you continue, Impulsive routes you through a brief intervention matched to what you said brought you there: box breathing for stress, a grounding exercise for boredom, a prompt to reach out for loneliness, a sleep alarm for fatigue. Then a gateway: walk away with clarity, or use a timed session.

TAPERING, NOT COLD TURKEY

Every Sunday evening, Impulsive checks in with you. It shows your week — urges intercepted, sessions used — and suggests reducing your session limit by one. You can accept or explain why not. Over weeks, the ceiling quietly drops.

This is not a blocker. It is a mirror.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

No account. No cloud. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. Your sessions, triggers, and progress live in an on-device database and nowhere else. You can export everything as JSON, back up with encrypted local storage, or delete it all instantly.

Impulsive uses Android's Usage Stats permission to detect when a monitored app is opened. That permission data is never stored or transmitted.

WHAT IMPULSIVE MONITORS

You choose which apps to monitor. Supported apps include Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X (Twitter), Snapchat, Reddit, YouTube, WhatsApp, Discord, Telegram, Netflix, Chrome, and more.

WHO THIS IS FOR

Impulsive is for people who already know they scroll too much and want a friction-based system — not a timer, not a badge, not a streak — to help them change. It is calm, non-judgmental, and built around the idea that awareness precedes change.

Impulsive does not moralize. It interrupts.
```
