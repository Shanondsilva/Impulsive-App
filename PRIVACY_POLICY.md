# Privacy Policy
**Effective Date:** April 2026

**1. Introduction**
Impulsive ("we", "our", or "the App") is a digital wellness application designed to help users manage impulsive app usage through active friction and behavioral redirection. We believe in "Privacy-first habit interruption. No tracking. No cloud. Just friction." This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to your privacy and how we handle data on your device.

**2. Data Collection and Storage**
Impulsive collects **NO personal data**. All data generated or processed by the App is stored exclusively and locally on your device. We do not transmit, sync, share, or store any of your data on external servers or cloud services. The App relies solely on local processing.

**3. Use of UsageStatsManager**
The App requests the `PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS` (Usage Access) permission.
*   **Purpose:** This permission is strictly necessary to detect when you open specific, user-selected applications that you have chosen to monitor.
*   **What we do NOT do:** We do not track your overall device usage, monitor how long you use apps, record what you do inside them, or report this usage to any server or third party. The API is used solely as a real-time local trigger to display the friction overlay (the "Intercept" screen) before you enter a monitored app.

**4. Third-Party SDKs and Telemetry**
Impulsive contains **no third-party tracking, analytics, telemetry, or advertising SDKs**. We do not monetize your data or behavior.

**5. User Control and Data Export**
Because your data belongs to you, Impulsive provides built-in tools for data management:
*   **Export:** You can export all your evaluation and historical data (interceptions, session choices, and weekly targets) as a JSON file directly from the App's Settings menu.
*   **Deletion:** Deleting the App or clearing its storage in your device settings will permanently erase all data associated with it. Since no data is backed up to our servers, this action is irreversible unless you have manually created a local encrypted backup file.

**6. Changes to this Privacy Policy**
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Effective Date." By continuing to use the App, you agree to the terms of the updated Policy.

**7. Contact**
For any questions regarding your privacy, please review the application documentation or contact the developer via the official repository or support channels.
