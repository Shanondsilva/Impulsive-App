# App Title
Impulsive: Impulse Control

# Short Description (80 chars max)
Privacy-first habit interruption. No tracking. No cloud. Just friction.

# Full Description (4000 chars max)
The only app that intervenes during the urge, not before or after.

Impulsive is a privacy-first digital wellness tool built on a simple premise: stopping doomscrolling isn't about blocking access entirely; it's about introducing active cognitive friction at the exact moment an urge strikes. 

Unlike most screen-time blockers that act like digital chastity belts or relying on guilt-inducing streak counters, Impulsive employs evidence-based psychological interventions to help you build real cognitive awareness. Streaks cause the Abstinence Violation Effect—the feeling of "I failed once, so I might as well keep going." Impulsive is designed with an anti-streak philosophy, focusing on the quality of your decisions today, not yesterday.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Active Friction: When you try to open a high-dopamine app, Impulsive intercepts you. To proceed, you must engage in a 15-second physical friction hold. This interrupts the automatic muscle-memory loop and gives your prefrontal cortex time to come online.
2. Trigger Routing: After the 15-second pause, you are asked to identify what triggered the urge (Bored, Stressed, Lonely, Tired, or Habit). Each trigger routes you to a specific, tailored intervention aimed at addressing the root cause, not just the symptom.
3. The Gateway: You are presented with a choice. You can Walk Away, or you can Continue Session. If you choose to continue, you enter a monitored 2-minute timeboxed session designed to fulfill the urge without letting it turn into an endless scroll.
4. Tapering Engine: Impulsive gradually tapers your allowed sessions week by week. It doesn’t demand instant perfection. It gently guides your neurochemistry toward baseline by systematically lowering your allowed interactions over time while ensuring you have a psychological path to success.

PRIVACY FIRST
Impulsive lives entirely on your device. 
- No telemetry or background analytics.
- No cloud syncing or external servers.
- No third-party tracking SDKs.
- All your behavioral data exists only on your local storage, and you can export or delete it completely at any time.

Regain control over your attention. Download Impulsive and put friction between your triggers and your habits.
